# Wave 3: deployability and safety

The lead was whether compute peaks correspond to deployable robot configurations.

Key result: the most useful constraints often came from module/reference-design and safety
documents rather than SoC headlines. Cooling, camera ingress, deterministic networking,
memory capacity, memory bandwidth, real-time cores, isolation, safety claim scope, and
software support must be modeled independently. Several platforms remain candidates only
after vendor disclosure or empirical board measurement closes their power/bandwidth gaps.
